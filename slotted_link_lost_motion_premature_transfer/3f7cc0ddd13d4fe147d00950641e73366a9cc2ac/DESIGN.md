# slotted_link_lost_motion_premature_transfer: mechanics and audit contract

The input pin has smooth prescribed displacement q. A nonpenetrating, one-sided end-stop constraint makes the normal output x=max(q-gap,0): a low-speed quasi-static displacement-controlled mechanism with negligible inertia. The pin begins at the open slot centre; rails hold the frame orientation. In CF only the output law changes to x=q even though the pin stays at the slot centre. This contact-free transfer is the sole impossible behavior. The real open slot, four connected feet and holed rail collars are procedural meshes; this is not a solid bar with a painted fake hole. The input pushrod stays below the frame and never supplies a hidden frame contact. No claim of native dynamics or resolved contact impulse is made.

## Source of motion
Low-speed displacement-controlled input and a unilateral rigid end stop. A resting output remains stationary until the pin reaches the slot end, then follows the continuous nonpenetrating contact constraint. The one CF operator makes the output follow the input without contact. No input or guide trajectory is changed.

## Temporal/causal contract
The native output has 120 samples at t=f/24 seconds. Onset is sampled independently from geometry and response. All ordinary parameters, motor/support input trajectories and camera motions are shared. The first changed RGB frame is discovered from actual pixels; the mask begins there. The CF operator is contact_free_slot_frame_transfer. Its consequences do not count as new independent operators.

## Static and rendered scale evidence
The 1,000,000 finite recipes vary four primary physical axes and two nuisance axes. 100,000 unique validated recipes is a CPU-only static target, not a claim of 100,000 rendered outputs. Rendered one-axis perturbations must show real image changes. Corner trajectories and every submitted formal seed must complete the stated outcomes. Failures must stop generation rather than be accepted by metadata alone.

## Required review evidence
Two native exact-head seed cases; all four decoded media streams per case; complete object-only mask replay; mechanics checks independent of the generator predicate; prompt and complete-motion inspection; native edge checks; runtime witness and case mapping; all artifact hashes; public playback and publication receipts. Source correctness and scale readiness are reported separately. No PASS is implied by this design document.
